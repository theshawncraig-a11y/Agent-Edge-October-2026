October AI Update

Alex Is Getting Smarter. And More Human.

This October marks the beginning of the next evolution of CINC AI.

Great agents know there’s a big difference between knowing what a lead wants and understanding why they want it. Alex is getting better at both.

With the launch of CINC’s new AI Nurture Engine, Alex will begin having deeper, more natural text conversations with new leads, asking more meaningful follow-up questions to uncover the motivations and goals behind their move.

And Alex isn’t going into those conversations blind. The Nurture Engine can use valuable context already inside CINC, including text conversations, website activity, listings a lead has viewed or favorited, agent notes, and other lead information, to create a clearer picture of the person on the other side of the conversation.

The impact could be significant. Based on testing of the new Nurture Engine AI model, CINC expects these improvements to drive approximately 25% more AI Agent‑Ready Leads, giving your team more opportunities to step into meaningful conversations with people who are ready for an agent.

Why That Matters

When it’s time for you to step in, the goal isn’t simply to hand you another lead. It’s to help you step into a better conversation, with more context around what that person needs, what matters to them, and what may motivate them to make a move.

Deeper conversations. Better context. And an expected 25% increase in AI Agent‑Ready Leads.

And this is only the first step. The Nurture Engine is designed to grow over time, expanding across more of the lead lifecycle and ultimately taking more of the day-to-day work of nurturing leads off your plate.

Alex is getting smarter, so you can spend more time doing what you do best: building relationships and closing deals.

The best CRM is the one you use.

Matt Mitchell leads Mitchell Home Group at Real in Central Ohio. After cycling through KV Core, Sierra Interactive, and Brivity, he came back to CINC for good — and now traces roughly 80% of his deals back to it.

01

CRM-hopping ends where the platform is complete.

Matt tried the rest before sticking with CINC for good. What made it stick was having the website, CRM, mobile app, AI, and ad spend all working together instead of stitched across separate tools.

02

Scripting that disarms the objection before it happens.

CINC’s training taught Matt to get ahead of buyer hesitation instead of reacting to it — in his words, disarming the lead “before they even have a chance to put up a wall.”

03

Results compound into a referral engine.

One $500K sale spawned an ongoing pipeline of referrals. One lead turned into three or four transactions. Another went from first contact to contract in 19 days. The largest deal — $1.275M cash — took an 11-month nurture to close. As Matt puts it: “The best CRM is the one you use. CINC is the one I use every day.”
